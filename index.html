      Optional University Application Workshop

      This workshop supplements the K–12 grade curriculum; it does not replace it. Independent practice, not a credential or an admissions-probability forecast. Universities assess grades, course prerequisites, academic work, recommendations, examinations where required and other criteria. This game does not replace any of them.

      1. Choose an academic pathway

      2. Baseline and retest

      Complete a 40-question practice diagnostic, with 10 questions in each of four broad domains. No hints, timer or rewards. Retake after study to measure progress. Scores are NOT standardized SAT/ACT scores.

      3. K–12 academic evidence and next skills

      Results count independently answered attempts, unique problems and study days. Strong in-game performance is NOT a verified examination result. Repeat skills on separate days and apply them in unfamiliar problems.

      4. Create substantial original work

      Writing, research and projects require human evaluation. Keep an evidence-based portfolio; saving text records authorship locally but does not grade it.

        5. Requirements outside the game

        Check the intended university and course: prerequisites, qualifications, deadlines, exams, writing, references, interviews, finance and genuine work outside the game. Admission standards change, and there is no universal acceptance score.

        How to Play

        🎮 Getting Started

        1. Create a profile or select an existing one

        2. Choose a grade level from the World Hub

        3. Select a game mode (Relaxed or Timed)

        4. Answer math questions to earn points and XP

        ⭐ Earning Rewards

        Points (⭐): Earned by answering questions correctly

        Crystals (💎): Earned from perfect games and streaks

        XP: Earned to level up and unlock new grades

        🏆 Progression System

        • Level up by earning XP

        • XP unlocks grades for practice, but does not certify academic mastery

        • Each grade requires two cumulative, no-hint checkpoints separated by at least seven days

        • Completing every grade checkpoint is a practice milestone, not an accredited qualification

        • Complete achievements for bonus rewards

        • Unlock all 13 grades from K to 12

        🐾 Pet System

        • Adopt pets from the shop

        • Visit the Pet Room to interact with them

        • Keep pets happy and fed

        • Level up pets through interactions

        🏪 Shop System

        • Spend points on avatar skins

        • Spend crystals on legendary items

        • Collect all pets and skins

        • Customize your character
